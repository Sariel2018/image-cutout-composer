# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want this project to be a respectful, practical, and technically rigorous collaboration space.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and specific in feedback.
- Focus on technical facts and reproducible cases.
- Assume good intent, but challenge weak assumptions with evidence.
- Keep discussions constructive and solution-oriented.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language.
- Repeated bad-faith arguments or intentional disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments/issues/PRs that violate this policy.

Serious or repeated violations may lead to temporary or permanent participation restrictions.
